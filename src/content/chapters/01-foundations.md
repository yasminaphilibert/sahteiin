Every good night in Beirut is punctuated by the same word. You say it over
arak, over coffee, over a glass of water at four in the morning: صحتين — two
healths. One for now, one for later. It is the only word in the language that
already contains this product's whole strategy — the 1AM stick and the 9AM
stick — and nobody owns it.

SAHTEIIN is that toast, bent just enough to be yours. The doubled I is not a
spelling mistake: it is the clink — two glasses meeting — drawn into the middle
of the word, and it is what makes a common toast registrable as a coined mark.
A name people already say every night, that no competitor can take back off
you.

---

This chapter is where we prove the foundation before anything gets drawn. A
kickoff session becomes a one-page brief — who picks this up, at 1AM and at
9AM, and why. The naming shortlist is read out loud in Arabic, French and
English, checked against a Beirut bar and a shelf abroad, and sanity-checked
for trademark before anyone falls in love. Then we pick the winner together.

The name on these pages is my recommendation, not a fait accompli — the
decision is the deliverable, and it is yours.
