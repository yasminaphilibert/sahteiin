One word, said twice a day — at 1AM over a bar counter, and at 9AM over a
kitchen counter. صحتين means two healths. This brand intends to earn both.

Seven cocktail flavours of electrolytes in a stick. The idea is good: hydration
with the manners of a bar, not a gym — premium, a little cheeky, at home in a
hotel minibar and never in a pharmacy aisle. What the idea needs now is the
brand around it: a name that is truly yours, a look that owns a shelf, and
packaging a printer can actually run.

This proposal is told in chapters. Each chapter is a phase of the work; each
phase closes with something you can hold and a quick sign-off from you. The
next chapter is revealed at each sign-off — so nothing big ever lands as a
surprise, and the story is only ever as far ahead as we both agree it should
be.
