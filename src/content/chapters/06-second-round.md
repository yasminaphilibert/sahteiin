Five chapters get the brand off the shelf. This one gets it off the shelf and
onto a phone — the toast still has to live somewhere after the bar closes.

Two routes, priced apart from the $6,500, because they answer different
questions. The brand site is where someone who has already met SAHTEIIN goes
to confirm it is real: the story, the range, the nearest place to buy it, in
English and Arabic. The shop is where they buy it without needing a nearest
place at all — seven flavours and the multipack, delivered across Lebanon,
paid for however they'd rather pay.

---

Both start the same way every phase in this proposal starts: mockups first,
your sign-off before a line of the site gets built. Neither is a commitment —
say yes to one, both, or neither, now or after week six, and the rest of this
proposal is unaffected either way.
