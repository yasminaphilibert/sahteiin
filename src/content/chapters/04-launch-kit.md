The campaign is one photograph told twice. The same hand, the same burgundy
sachet — raised over a crowded bar at 1AM in warm tungsten, and standing on a
sunlit kitchen counter at 9AM. Say it before. Say it after. Documentary grain,
real skin, no studio gloss: the product photographed the way the night
actually looks.

Every asset in the launch kit is a variation on that split: the 9:16 social
templates, the launch posts, the shot list for your first real photography.
One idea, repeated until it belongs to you.

---

This chapter closes the project the responsible way: a ~12-page mini guideline
that keeps the brand consistent after I leave the room, and an organised
handover of every working file — fonts licensed in your name, logos in every
format, dielines your printer can run. The brand becomes yours in the fullest
sense: on final payment, you own all of it.
