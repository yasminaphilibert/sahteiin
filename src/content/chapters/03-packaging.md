Seven cocktails, each name bent by one sound: HUGO AGAIN, SALTY RITA,
LIMON-CHILL, PALOMA MIA, BLUE HULA, BRAMBLE ON, NOGRONI. The bend always
points the same way — at the next round, or at the morning after. A smaller
second line carries the facts (which cocktail, which fruit); the big name gets
to have the personality.

The colours are rebuilt across the whole wheel — green, lime, gold, coral,
azure, violet, burgundy — so no two sticks can be confused at arm's length.
That is the test the shelf has to pass: seven you can spot across the room,
across a bar, across a supermarket aisle. Each sachet is one flat colour, its
name set huge and cropped by the crimp, its icon drawn large and tone-on-tone
in a darker cut of its own colour.

Behind the pictures, this chapter is production work: bilingual labels with
